# Landing Page

- Scope: public Sensa introduction shown before authentication, plus a signed-in preview route.
- Visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: people who already possess lived experience and meaning, and want another language to enter that life.
- Job: understand Sensa's belief, see how the learning path begins, and choose to enter the existing email sign-in flow.
- Content constraint: no competitive comparison, feature grid, testimonials, metrics, pricing or invented outcome claims.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page owns one idea: “你已经拥有一个世界。” It makes learning visible as a connection from lived context to recognized meaning to expression. It refuses the category-default marketing stack of feature cards, proof logos and exaggerated outcomes.

**OWN-WORLD:** A committed four-color Sensa field: Paper for reading, Forest for a large semantic specimen, Sage for connective signals, Stone for structure. The composition is a split pamphlet with generous paper rules, editorial language type and restrained product sans. Geometry is mostly open and flat; one bounded specimen earns its radius.

**STORY:** The visitor first receives the belief, then sees one concrete Context–Sense–Expression example, reads why language learning does not begin from emptiness, follows the real three-part way of using Sensa, and reaches an invitation to enter. Product facts remain grounded in the existing Web and extension flows.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim header holds the fixed color Logo, one “how it works” anchor, language control and a large login action. Below, the left field carries the full thesis and primary action; the right Forest field presents one lived scene becoming a meaning and then an English expression. A single Sage thread draws those three states together without hiding their copy.

**FORM:** Split pamphlet plus semantic thread, the assigned fourth structure from the grounded surface list. Seed key `6e065cbe`. Button scale is large; desktop is an asymmetric two-zone spread, mobile becomes one intentional reading column with the action before the specimen.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
